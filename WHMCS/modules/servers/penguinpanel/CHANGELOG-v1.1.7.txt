PenguinPanel WHMCS Server Module v1.1.7

- Privacy-safe PenguinPanel usernames.
- No longer uses the WHMCS client email address as the default PenguinPanel username.
- Reuses an existing non-email WHMCS service Username when supplied.
- Otherwise generates a stable `penguin-<clientId>` login.
- The returned PenguinPanel login is saved to the WHMCS service Username field as before.
- Existing already-created PenguinPanel accounts are not automatically renamed.
